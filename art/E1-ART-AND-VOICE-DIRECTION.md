# 600 BILLION — Edition One Art & Voice Direction

## The one-sentence concept

**600 BILLION is a positive cypherpunk world where the future already works:
friends use Bitcoin, keys, signals, power and timelocks to build useful open
infrastructure — with confidence, humor and a little YOLO.**

This is not a dystopia with a Bitcoin sticker. The black and ultraviolet palette gives
the game edge; the world itself stays warm, capable and alive. Cypherpunk means freedom
technology in daylight.

## Emotional promise

Every card should make the player feel at least one of these:

- **I can build this.**
- **I can own this.**
- **I can understand this.**
- **We can make the network stronger together.**
- **This is serious technology made by people who still know how to have fun.**

Even disruptive mechanics depict adaptation, routing, repair, verification or a bold
competitive move. They do not turn the setting into war, panic or despair.

## World5

Open protocols are the lived environment of the game:

- Bitcoin is native money and durable settlement.
- Keys give people ownership and agency.
- Signal connects peers without asking permission.
- Power turns physical work into useful capacity.
- Timelocks turn patience into certainty.
- Nostr is the social layer: identity lives with the player, not a platform.

The world is full of rooftop workshops, local energy, open tools, orchards, radio
links, hardware wallets, block clocks, coffee, plants, markets and friends shipping
things that work.

## Visual language

- **Core:** matte black, dense and premium.
- **Primary energy:** Bitcoin orange, warm and optimistic.
- **Cypherpunk accent:** ultraviolet purple, precise and electric.
- **Light:** sunrise, golden hour, optimistic noon, festival dusk or warm workshop light.
- **Material:** blackened steel, copper, timber, ceramic, glass, solar panels, cables and
  real open hardware.
- **Image:** one clear subject, one legible action, coherent depth and calm crop edges.
- **Texture:** cinematic editorial realism with restrained screenprint grain.

Characters always come from their canonical `join.600.wtf` **Detailed ·front**
reference. Their species, face, proportions, wardrobe, materials, palette and signature
accessories remain consistent. They belong inside the scene; they never look pasted on.

## Voice

Card rules are short, literal and readable. Flavor is compact, collectible and playful.
It can be brave, playful or cheeky, but never corporate.

Good:

- Builders brought tests, snacks and exactly one spare antenna.
- YOLO, but verify.
- No permission. Just builders.
- Keys ready. Future open.
- Ship the signal.
- The next block is ours to build.

Avoid:

- price promises or investment advice;
- corporate Web3 language;
- generic hacker clichés;
- nihilism, collapse fantasy or helplessness;
- long educational copy on the physical card.

Protocol explanations, beginner help, sources and deeper learning live in the website
UI as metadata. The collectible card face keeps only gameplay information and one cool
flavor line.

## Five resource moods

| Resource | Meaning | Visual mood |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power | work, heat, speed, mining | orange current, microgrids, useful heat |
| Bitcoin | value, growth, scarcity, settlement | orchards, markets, nodes, abundance |
| Keys | ownership, privacy, signatures | tactile devices, independent stations, violet light |
| Signal | people, Nostr, coordination | rooftops, radios, antennas, sunrise |
| Timelock | patience, sequence, certainty | block clocks, vaults, deep purple calm |

## Hard exclusions

- no text, logos, UI or card frames inside standalone artwork;
- no floating rings, wireframe polygons or meaningless crypto glyphs;
- no repeated procedural patterns;
- no collage, sticker edge, rectangular halo or crude cutout;
- no generic hooded hacker;
- no weapons-first staging, grimdark lighting or cyberpunk doom;
- no invented background cast on character cards.

## Production lock

- Standalone artwork: **1920 × 2400 px**, 4:5 portrait.
- Card layout: orange outer frame, black core, purple structure, cream title bar.
- Artwork: deliberate inner border and a shorter crop that leaves room for the UI.
- Rules: target **three lines maximum** on the card face.
- Learning text: website/game metadata only.
- Character reference source: canonical `join.600.wtf` Detailed ·front files.
