600B story cut — regeneration prompts (Imagine → Video, 6 seconds each)
========================================================================

Why regenerate: post-compositing the 600B mark onto moving AI footage fights
the medium. These runs animate PRE-BRANDED stills (make them with
`uv run python brand_stills.py` → stills-branded/), so the video model itself
keeps the emblem glued to the prop. Attach the branded still, paste the
prompt, 6 seconds, CAMERA ONLY, no Extend.

Every prompt ends with the standing rule:
"Keep the chest emblem exactly as shown in the reference image — it is part
of the costume. No text anywhere else. No new characters."

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROLOG P1 (new footage, the narrator's opening — Satoshi is gone)
Still: stills/01-world-bitcoin.png (unbranded, no characters)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Slow push through the dusk orchard of servers toward one empty workbench
under a single warm lamp. A coat rack stands without a coat; a cold mug.
The glowing glass cubes along the roots brighten one by one, keeping time.
Golden hour, soot black, no people, no text. The absence is the subject.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROLOG P2 (new footage — but he left us the timechain)
Still: stills/07-next-block.jpg
----------------------------------------------------------------
Slow dolly along the line of glowing clock cubes as paper tape feeds from
one into the next; gears turn, pendulums sway. Each cube lights the moment
the tape reaches it — an unbroken chain into the distance. No people,
no text.
