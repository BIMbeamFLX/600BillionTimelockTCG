Rulebook Cards

Play locally

Two-player hotseat on one machine, with all 295 Edition One cards. Each seat gets a randomised 40-card Stack in the rulebook's recommended prototype shape. The engine runs the turn structure, Buffer and resource burn, costs, clash and state checks; cards whose text it cannot fully model are flagged ! and resolved by a bounded, attributed proposal your opponent can accept or reject.

Want your own 40? Build and save Stacks in the Stack Builder — they appear in both seat menus here.

Or play across the table

One referee runs the rules and deals both seats; every play travels over a socket, and only the invite, the accept and the signed result ever touch a relay. Sign in to be named — or stay anonymous, it changes nothing about the game.
