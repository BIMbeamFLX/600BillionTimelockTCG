The proof sheet

All 296 shipped card faces plus the shared back — the files themselves, never a re-drawing. Each face below is the exact render written by scripts/render_card_pngs.py, the single authority on card geometry: the per-card art rectangle, the ink-anchored title ladder and the text plate are its decisions, recorded in art/cards/node-runner-web/face-geometry.json and shipped as these bitmaps. This page lays the renders out and prints each card's locked data beside its face, so a proof pass checks the render against the text with no layer in between that could drift. Web faces are trimmed to the 63 × 88 mm cut line at 300 dpi; print masters keep the full 3 mm bleed in art/cards/node-runner-print/. All 295 catalog cards →
