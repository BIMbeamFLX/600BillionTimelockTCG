The Arena is now the table.

This URL used to hold a static picture of the board. The board it was picturing is real now, so the picture was only going to drift out of date. Everything it proposed lives in the game itself:

  • Arced zones — your Network bends toward you, theirs bends away.
  • The shared clash lane — attacks meet in the middle of the table.
  • Committed cards turned sideways — the same tell as a paper game.
  • Attack and block arrows — ember to the player, violet to the attacker.
  • The uptime dial — both seats, always visible, counting down from 20.
