Quick start · Edition One

Play in 5 minutes.

Six steps, real cards, no rules lawyering. The full rulebook is there when you want the fine print — you do not need it to win your first game.

Start a game vs the NPC Full rulebook
STEP 1

Take them to zero

Both players start at 20 Uptime. Bring your opponent to 0 and you win. That is the whole victory condition — everything below is just how you get there.

Your Uptime is the number in the chip square next to your name at the table.

Power Bitcoin Keys Signal Timelock

Five Resources. A Stack usually leans on one or two of them.

STEP 2

One Resource per turn, free

Once each turn you may put a Resource from your Wallet onto your Network. It costs nothing and cannot be answered. This is your economy — miss it and you fall a turn behind.

STEP 3

Commit it to pay for things

A Resource on your Network does nothing until you commit it — click it and it turns sideways, putting one Resource into your Buffer. The Buffer is what pays printed costs.

A junction like this one asks which Resource you want; a plain Power Plant just makes Power.

Toni China, Rough Miner costs 1P — one Power plus one of anything. So: commit two Resources, then play her.

STEP 4

Spend it before the phase ends

The one rule that bites beginners: your Buffer empties at the end of every phase, and each unspent Resource costs you 1 Uptime. Generate what you are about to spend — not more. That is resource burn (§12.1), and yes, it hurts.

An Avatar's two numbers are Action (damage it deals) and Resilience (damage it survives). It arrives with Boot Delay: it cannot attack on the turn it lands.

STEP 5

Clash

In the Clash phase, every Avatar that may legally attack pulses ember. Click the ones you want to send, then press Continue — an arrow shows where each attack is going.

Your opponent picks blockers: first the attacker, then their own Avatar. Unblocked attackers hit the player, and their Action comes straight off that player's Uptime.

Avatars that took damage equal to their Resilience are decommissioned into the Archive.

STEP 6

The table does the rest

You never have to memorise the eight phases. Continue walks the turn forward and stops whenever you actually have a decision; End turn hands over. The line at the top always says where you are and what you can do.

Left click — acts. Play the card, commit the Resource, declare the attack.
Right click — explains. Opens the card window with its full text.

Cards the engine cannot fully script are marked ! and are resolved by a proposal your opponent accepts or rejects. Rare, and always visible.
