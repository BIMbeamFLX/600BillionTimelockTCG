1 · Agree

Both players confirm the stake during match setup. It is recorded in the signed match transcript.

2 · Play

The match runs peer-to-peer over WebRTC. Both clients run the same deterministic engine — the result is provable.

3 · Zap

After the win, the app opens the zap to the winner's Lightning address (NIP-57). Honor system — your reputation plays too.

4 · Stats

Both players publish the result as a Nostr event. Settled duels build your public track record.
