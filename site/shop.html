How the real thing will work

1 · Pay yourself

The mint hands you a Lightning invoice. Your wallet pays it — this site never sees a key, a card number or a balance.

2 · The box pulls

A settled payment claims the next card off the same committed box. Same order, same odds, no re-roll on the operator's side.

3 · It is yours

The card is issued as a signed event and the art is content-addressed on Blossom — the hash is the address, so any mirror serves the same bytes.
