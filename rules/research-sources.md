# E1 rules research and provenance

The public rulebook uses original 600B wording. These sources were used only to verify
the historical mechanical envelope and document project risk.

## Primary and official references

- [The Original Magic Rulebook — Wizards transcription preserved by Petr
  Hudeček](https://hudecekpetr.cz/other/rulebooks/rulebook-alpha-1993/index.html)
- [25 More Random Things About Magic — official 40-card and unrestricted-copy
  history](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/25-more-random-things-about-magic-2016-06-20)
- [The History of Tokens, Part 1 — official Alpha token
  history](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/the-history-of-tokens-part-1)
- [Wizards of the Coast Fan Content
  Policy](https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy)

The Fan Content Policy is not treated as permission to publish a separate game. It
expressly limits the use of Wizards intellectual property in other games. The 600B
prototype therefore uses original names, prose, art, icons, frame design and trade dress,
and still requires independent intellectual-property review before public or commercial
release.

## 600B sources of truth

- [600Billion lore and member index](https://join.600.wtf)
- [600 000 000 000 repository](https://github.com/600-000-000-000/600000000000)
- `600B Design System — Print.pdf`

Character identity follows the live `join.600.wtf` cast. The design system controls logo,
palette, typography and voice. TCG-specific rules and terminology are versioned in the
Edition One rulebook.
